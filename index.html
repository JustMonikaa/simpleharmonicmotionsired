Trig Function:
The Sine ($\sin\theta$) is the vertical projection of the block's position, forming the opposite leg of the right triangle.
The Cosine ($\cos\theta$) is the horizontal projection of the block's position, forming the adjacent leg of the right triangle.
The Tangent ($\tan\theta$) is the line segment tangent to the circle at the current angle, extending until it intersects the x-axis.
The Secant ($\sec\theta$) comes from the Latin secare (to cut). It is the segment extending from the origin to intersect the tangent line, "cutting" through the circle.
The Cosecant ($\csc\theta$) is the segment on the y-axis extending from the origin to where it intersects the tangent line.
The Cotangent ($\cot\theta$) is the line segment tangent to the circle at the current angle, extending until it intersects the y-axis.
